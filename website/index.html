02 — Formats

It plays what you have.

Files are identified by what is inside them, not by what the extension claims. Every format below is indexed, tagged, seekable and gapless.

  • .mp3 MPEG audio The one everybody has — read, tagged and seekable like everything else.
  • .flac FLAC Lossless, decoded as-is with no resampling beyond what the hardware requires.
  • .wav PCM · ADPCM (RIFF) Uncompressed, and left that way.
  • .aiff PCM (AIFF) Apple's uncompressed container, read natively.
  • .caf PCM (CAF) Core Audio Format, straight off the disk.
  • .ogg .oga Ogg Vorbis Patent-free then, properly handled now.
  • .m4a .m4b AAC · ALAC in MP4 Both the lossy and the lossless side, audiobook chapters included.
  • .opus Ogg Opus The usual decoder library has no Opus at all; ours is pure Rust.
  • .aac Raw AAC · ADTS A hand-written demuxer, so bare .aac streams index and seek.
  • .dsf .dff DSD64 · DSD128 · DSD256 Decoded in software to PCM, so any DAC can play it — no special driver.
  • Not supported Said plainly APE · WavPack · TAK · Musepack · WMA · Speex · RealAudio — and CUE sheets are not split into tracks.

Where your library lives

%APPDATA%\com.localmusicplayer.desktop
A SQLite database in WAL mode, plus a cover cache addressed by content hash.

Your music

Exactly where you left it
Oberon indexes folders in place. It does not move, copy or rename a single file.

Signed and self-updating

The installer is signed, and every update is verified against a published key before it is applied. You can ignore updates forever without losing anything but the newest fixes.

No account, ever

There is nothing to sign into and nothing to sign up for. The only outbound request the application makes is the update check — and you can decline it permanently.
